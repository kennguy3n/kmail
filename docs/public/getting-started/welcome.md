KMail is private, encrypted business email and calendar for teams. It
runs on your own domain and speaks open standards (JMAP, IMAP, SMTP,
CalDAV, CardDAV), so you can use our web app or any standards-compliant
client.

## What you get

- **Mail** — encrypted mailboxes, shared inboxes, scheduled and undo
  send, snooze, and Confidential Send.
- **Calendar & contacts** — CalDAV calendars, shared and resource
  calendars, CardDAV contacts, and a tenant-wide Global Address List.
- **Privacy controls** — zero-access vaults, customer-managed keys
  (Privacy plan), TOTP and passkey sign-in.
- **Admin tooling** — a DNS onboarding wizard, deliverability and DMARC
  reporting, retention policies, and SCIM provisioning.

## The 5-minute setup

1. **Sign up** and choose a plan (Core, Pro, or Privacy).
2. **Add your domain** and complete the [DNS setup](/help/getting-started/dns-setup)
   with the guided wizard.
3. **Create your first user** (this becomes your admin account).
4. **Connect a client** — the [web app](/mail), [Thunderbird](/help/email-setup/thunderbird),
   or [Apple Mail](/help/email-setup/apple-mail).
5. **Migrate** existing mail from [Gmail](/help/migration/gmail-migration)
   or [Microsoft 365](/help/migration/m365-migration) (Pro and Privacy).

## Next steps

- Set up [shared inboxes](/help/admin/shared-inboxes) for addresses like
  `support@` or `sales@`.
- Understand [DMARC](/help/security/dmarc-explained) to protect your
  domain from spoofing.
- Review [plans and billing](/help/billing/plans-and-billing).
