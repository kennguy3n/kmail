KMail is designed for people who need reliable email and calendar without giving their data to an ad-supported platform. This post is a quick visual tour of the screens a typical business user sees in their first week.

## Inbox and triage

The inbox is split into a sidebar of mailboxes and a message list. You can switch mailboxes, apply labels, snooze messages, or mark them as priority. The "Priority" view surfaces the messages the system thinks need attention first.

![KMail inbox](/screenshots/01-mail-inbox.png)

Keyboard shortcuts keep power users moving: `c` to compose, `g i` for inbox, `g c` for calendar, `/` for search, and `?` for the help modal.

## Compose and send

The composer supports rich text, plain text, and signatures. You can attach files, schedule a send for later, or switch to confidential mode for a password-protected, expiring message.

![KMail compose](/screenshots/02-compose.png)

## Calendar and events

The calendar shows your personal and team calendars side by side. Toggle calendars on and off, create events from a free slot, and respond to invitations.

![KMail calendar](/screenshots/06-calendar.png)

Creating an event is a single form with title, time, participants, and calendar selection.

![KMail event creation](/screenshots/07-event-create.png)

## Contacts and shared address book

Contacts lists your personal address book and the tenant-wide Global Address List. When you type a recipient in compose, KMail suggests matching contacts automatically.

![KMail contacts](/screenshots/09-contacts.png)

## Shared inboxes and protected folders

Teams can share an inbox so multiple people can triage support or sales mail without sharing passwords. Protected folders add an access log and explicit grants so you know exactly who read what.

![KMail shared inbox](/screenshots/04-shared-inbox.png)

![KMail protected folders](/screenshots/05-protected-folders.png)

## From zero to first email

For a new domain, the setup flow is:

1. Sign up and pick a plan.
2. Add your domain in the admin console.
3. Run the DNS wizard to copy MX, SPF, DKIM, and DMARC records.
4. Add users.
5. Start sending.

![KMail DNS wizard](/screenshots/12-dns-wizard.png)

If you are migrating from Gmail or Microsoft 365, the migration admin walks you through connecting the old account and moving mail in the background.
