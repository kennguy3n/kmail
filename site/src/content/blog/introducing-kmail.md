Email is still the backbone of how businesses communicate — and for most
teams it runs on platforms that monetize attention and treat your
messages as data to mine. We think a company's most sensitive
conversations deserve better.

**KMail is private, encrypted business email and calendar for teams.** It
runs on your own domain, speaks open standards (JMAP, IMAP, SMTP, CalDAV,
CardDAV), and is built so the people who operate it can't quietly read
your mail.

## What makes it different

- **Privacy by design.** Per-tenant encryption, zero-access vaults, and
  optional customer-managed keys. No ads, no content scanning for
  marketing, no training models on your mail.
- **Built for teams.** Shared inboxes that don't cost a seat, delegated
  calendars, a global address list, and SCIM provisioning.
- **No lock-in.** Use our fast web app or any standards-compliant client.
  Export everything, anytime.

## Who it's for

Small and mid-sized businesses that want the polish of Gmail or Microsoft
365 without the surveillance — and the controls (CMK, retention, DMARC
reporting) that compliance-minded teams need.
